# Hardware & Deployment

## Model Selection
- NE301: imaging-first (long distance / small digits)
- NE101: battery-powered, ultra-low-frequency reading

## Installation
- Lens-to-dial distance/angle, fill light, weatherproof housing

## Power & Network
- Battery vs reading frequency; Wi-Fi / Cat.1 choice

## Watch-outs
- Dirty dials, glare, condensation
