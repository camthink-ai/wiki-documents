NeoEyes NE301 (AI Camera)

New low-power Edge AI camera based on STM32N6

High-performance Cortex-M55 + NPU architecture, supporting local real-time inference. Modular design adaptable to various lenses and sensors, ideal for computer vision and AIoT nodes.

NeoEyes NE101 (IoT Node)

Ultra-low power visual sensor node based on ESP32-S3

Designed for battery-powered scenarios, supporting Wi-Fi/Cat.1/Wi-Fi HaLow connectivity. Minimalist architecture, millisecond wake-up capture from deep sleep, suitable for outdoor monitoring and meter reading.
